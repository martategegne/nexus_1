My Work Journey

My work journey is a continuous story of learning, leadership, service, technology, and personal growth. From my early school experiences to university, volunteering, fellowships, community leadership, and software development, every experience has contributed to the person and professional I am becoming.

My Journey at a Glance

My journey has developed across several interconnected areas: education, leadership, community service, professional exposure, technology, and continuous self-development.

  • Education: From elementary education to Software Engineering at Addis Ababa University.
  • Leadership: Student leadership, class representation, club leadership, and community responsibilities.
  • Community Service: Volunteer activities and initiatives focused on contributing to my school and wider community.
  • Professional Exposure: Practical IT experience and workplace learning.
  • Fellowship: Millennium Fellowship Class of 2025 and continued alumni engagement.
  • Technology: Software development, web development, mobile development, backend development, databases, networking, and cybersecurity.
